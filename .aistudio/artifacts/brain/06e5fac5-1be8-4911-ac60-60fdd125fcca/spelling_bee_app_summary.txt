================================================================================
U.E.P. SALTO ÁNGEL SPELLING BEE 2026 — STAGE ROULETTE & PRESENTATION SYSTEM
================================================================================
Target Audience: AI Language Models, Prompt Context, Contest AV Directors, Judges
Purpose: Complete functional, structural, and technical application specification

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. APPLICATION OVERVIEW & MISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This application is a specialized, production-ready stage presentation and
interactive word-roulette platform developed specifically for the
"U.E.P. Salto Ángel Spelling Bee 2026" annual English spelling championship.

The application is projected live onto large auditorium TVs, stage LED video
walls, and 1080p/4K projectors in front of students, parents, judges, and
pronouncers. It controls the high-stakes tiebreaker rounds where spellers spin
the wheel on stage to determine their target spelling word and must spell it
before a strict countdown timer expires.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STAGE HARDWARE & AUDITORIUM OPERATING CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Display Medium: Large TV screens, auditorium projectors, or LED video walls.
- Aspect Ratio: Strictly locked to 16:9 (`aspect-video`, `max-w-[calc(100vh*16/9)]`)
  to prevent screen distortion, edge clipping, or letterboxing on stage TVs.
- Navigation Ergonomics:
  * Top and bottom Genially-style navigation bars auto-hide during presentation
    to maximize stage presence and eliminate visual clutter.
  * Side slide navigation buttons are strictly 48x48px circular buttons centered
    vertically at `top-1/2 -translate-y-1/2`. They never intercept Y-axis clicks,
    ensuring operators never trigger accidental slide transitions when clicking
    the wheel or stage background.
  * Keyboard shortcut: Press `Ctrl+Enter` or `Space` anywhere to spin the wheel.
- Audio Discipline (Auditorium & Microphone Safe):
  * Crisp mechanical peg ticking sound during spin synthesized via Web Audio API.
  * Countdown timer warning ticks and a buzzer at zero seconds.
  * Text-to-speech voice reading and celebratory trumpet fanfare upon landing are
    deliberately muted/disabled by default so they do not cause loud acoustic
    feedback or interfere with stage microphones and the human pronouncer.
  * Visual confetti animation triggers gracefully without audio blast.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CONTEST STRUCTURE & CATEGORY WHEEL HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The competition is divided into two primary groups with zero bloat in naming:

A. ELEMENTARY ("Elementary")
   - Grades included: 1st Grade, 2nd Grade, 3rd Grade, 4th Grade, 5th Grade, 6th Grade.
   - Wheel Structure: Each individual grade has its own distinct, unique wheels:
     * 1st Place Tiebreaker Wheel: Default timer of 40 seconds.
     * 2nd & 3rd Place Tiebreaker Wheel: Default timer of 20 seconds.
   - Visual Theme: Royal Blue & Golden Yellow celebratory stage background with
     school crest banner and stars.

B. HIGH SCHOOL ("High School")
   - Years included: 1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year, 5th Year.
   - Wheel Structure: Rather than individual wheels per year, High School utilizes
     two unified, comprehensive stage tiebreaker wheels for all 1st to 5th years:
     * High School - 1st Place: 40 seconds countdown timer.
     * High School - 2nd/3rd Place: 20 seconds countdown timer.
   - Dynamic Background: Clicking High School instantly swaps the stage background
     to the official High School 2026 backdrops:
     - Slide 0 (Welcome): `480b2e32-4249-44c9-83bd-7e91fc5cbdba_0097f3f2.jpg`
       (Features "Spelling Bee High School 2026" crest, bees, and ribbons).
     - Slide 1 (Roulette Wheel): `8d019f05-30e0-4935-904c-e42987682443_a0014e1b.jpg`
       (Features clean open dark blue auditorium center for the wheel).

C. NAMING CONVENTIONS & ZERO BLOAT DISCIPLINE
   - No excessive words: We use "Elementary" (never "Elementary Division") and
     "High School".
   - Wheels are named cleanly: "1st Grade - 1st Place", "1st Grade Tie",
     "High School - 1st Place", "High School Tie".
   - Redundant timer suffixes like "(40s)" and "(20s)" are strictly excluded from
     dropdown menus, tabs, and buttons.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. KEY INTERACTIVE COMPONENTS & FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRESENTATION DECK (3 Slides):
   - Slide 0: Welcome banner with animated school shield, bee mascots, and
     category indicators.
   - Slide 1: Stage Roulette with active category header, high-DPI canvas wheel,
     countdown timer with digital display, and quick-action controls.
   - Slide 2: Champion celebration hall of fame slide.

2. HIGH-DPI CANVAS ROULETTE ENGINE:
   - Built with HTML5 Canvas 2D supporting 4K retina displays (`window.devicePixelRatio`).
   - Realistic quintic easing (`easeOutQuint`) for dramatic deceleration.
   - Right-side needle indicator with physics spring deflection as slice pegs pass.
   - Center metallic hubcap with clickable "SPIN" button.
   - Subtle idle rotation when stationary to maintain high stage energy.
   - Custom slice weighting, disabling, per-slice colors, and images.

3. WINNER MODAL & INTEGRATED COUNTDOWN TIMER:
   - When the wheel lands on a word, a large, bold, single-line stage banner displays
     the winning word with sparkling glow.
   - Options to:
     * Start the countdown timer immediately (40s for 1st place, 20s for 2nd/3rd).
     * Remove the winning word from the wheel so it cannot be selected again.
     * Close the modal and spin again.

4. WHEEL CREATOR STUDIO:
   - Full-featured management studio based on `wheel_of_names_studio_ad4d0835.html`.
   - Hidden Dropdown Wheel Selector: Replaces horizontal scrolling clutter with a
     clean popup menu showing all wheels organized by category.
   - Delete/Remove Wheel Action: Operators can easily delete any unnecessary wheel
     via the red "Remove Wheel" button or the trash icon in the menu, guarded by
     a confirmation dialog and a minimum safety limit (preventing zero wheels).
   - Word List Editor: Multi-line textarea or individual card entries with instant
     color assignment, slice weights, toggle switches, and bulk shuffle/sort.
   - 3-Tab Customizer:
     * During Spin: Duration (1-30s), tick sound pitch, max visible slices.
     * After Spin: Confetti toggle, popup modal, fanfare volume.
     * Appearance: Color palettes (Classic, Pastel, Neon Cyber, Sunset, Ocean,
       Monochrome), slice border lines, center hub image upload.
   - Import/Export: Backup and restore wheels via JSON.
   - Document Analyzer (AI-Powered): Upload Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx) files;
     Gemini AI automatically parses words, strips Spanish translations and verb
     conjugations, and constructs ready-to-spin wheels.

5. LOCAL NETWORK PERSISTENCE & MULTI-DEVICE SYNC:
   - Built-in Node/Express backend (`server.ts`) with `/api/wheels` endpoint.
   - Any edits made in the studio on one PC or tablet instantly persist to
     `data/wheels.json` and sync across other machines on the same local network.
   - LocalStorage backup ensures offline operation even if Wi-Fi drops in the auditorium.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Framework: React 19 (TypeScript) + Vite
- Styling: Tailwind CSS (modern `@import "tailwindcss";` pipeline)
- Backend: Express running in `server.ts` on port 3000
- AI Engine: `@google/genai` TypeScript SDK (server-side Gemini API proxy)
- State Management: React useState / useEffect with localStorage + `/api/wheels` sync
- Audio Synthesis: Web Audio API (zero external audio file dependencies)
- Icons: `lucide-react`
